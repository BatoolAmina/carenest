## Shared visual direction

Use a cinematic, premium healthcare look: deep emerald (#061b16), forest green (#0f7652), warm champagne gold (#d9b45f), soft ivory highlights, elegant architectural lighting, subtle atmospheric haze, and generous dark negative space. Keep images realistic and calm, with no readable text, logos, watermarks, distorted hands, or identifiable patient information. Use a consistent 16:10 or 4:3 crop and leave some quiet space for text overlays.

